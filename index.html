    Help

    How FringeFile works, in plain English.

    Who signs the Statement of Compliance?

    The owner — always. The Statement of Compliance is signed under penalty of perjury, so FringeFile generates it unsigned and never signs, certifies, or submits anything. The weekly flow is: enter hours → run the auto-check → build the packet → the owner reviews it, signs the statement, and submits the packet through the GC's portal (LCPtracker, eComply, Elation — whichever the GC uses). "Mark submitted" in FringeFile only records that the owner did that.

    How does the fringe split work?

    Every classification in a wage determination has a base rate and a fringe rate. The fringe can be delivered to bona fide plans (health, pension, training), paid in cash on the check, or split between the two — what matters is that plan + cash at least equals the required fringe for every hour. Each worker's plan and cash amounts live on the Crew screen; the auto-check flags anyone whose split falls short, by the hour and by the week.

    How is overtime figured?

    Hours past 40 in the payroll week are paid at 1.5× the worker's rate. FringeFile fills straight time first, in day order, and treats the rest as overtime — the WH-347 preview shows the straight-time and overtime rows separately, the way the form wants them. Fringe is owed on all hours, straight and overtime alike, at the same hourly fringe rate.

    What do the auto-check findings mean?

    Errors are things that would make the certified payroll wrong: a rate below the determination, a fringe split that falls short, hours reported in a classification the worker isn't registered under, an unregistered apprentice, a classification with no rate on file, or deductions larger than the gross. A packet with errors can't be marked ready and prints as DRAFT. Warnings — like an apprentice ratio over the demo's 1-per-3 rule — don't block, but the owner should read them before signing, because the applicable program's own ratio governs.

    Where does my data live? How do I back it up?

    In this demo edition, everything lives on this device, in this browser — no account, nothing uploaded. Export the payroll register and submission log CSVs from the Submissions screen to keep dated audit copies, and use Reset below to restore the fictional sample data or Clear to empty the app. Clearing your browser's site data erases your records, so export before you do.

    What's not in the demo?

    CSV hours import (the full build reads your payroll export instead of typing), additional state forms beyond the Oklahoma pattern, and portal-file formats beyond the print packet. The demo types hours and prints the federal packet so the math, the checks, and the prepare-you-certify flow can be judged on their own.
